# Scarlet Vale

**Role:** Hero — aerialist, co-star of the circus
**IQ: 164** — *Brilliant, inventive, extremely fast thinker.*

Scarlet Vale has no memory of parents or family. Adopted by a circus owner in Romania, she grew up beneath the big top and learned the trapeze before most kids learned to stay out of trouble.

After the Great Depression, the circus crossed the Atlantic for Canada, where Scarlet became its fearless star aerialist. Brilliant, rebellious and almost impossible to intimidate, she eventually met the one performer even harder to control than herself: Balthazhar.

She never tried to tame him. She treated him as a partner. He approved.
