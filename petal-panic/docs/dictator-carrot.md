# Dictator Carrot

**Role:** Villain — criminal mastermind (self-proclaimed)
**IQ: 23** — *Somehow commands an army despite being spectacularly stupid.*

Wanted across America for an impressive collection of crimes, failed schemes and suspicious botanical experiments, Dictator Carrot is a self-proclaimed criminal mastermind with one modest ambition: absolute domination.

His latest invention, an experimental botanical weapon, finds the perfect testing ground in a travelling Canadian circus. One bloom later, the big top is overrun by mutated plants, corrupted performers and vegetable henchmen.

Unfortunately for Carrot, his plan has one minor weakness: **Carrot himself.**
