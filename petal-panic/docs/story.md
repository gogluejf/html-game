# Petal Panic — Story

Scarlet Vale and Balthazhar are the star duo of a travelling Canadian circus. When Colonel Carrot, an American criminal mastermind with questionable intelligence, unleashes an experimental botanical weapon beneath the big top, the circus erupts into **Petal Panic**. Plants mutate, performers turn hostile, and the entire circus becomes a living nightmare. Scarlet and Balthazhar have to fight through it together and put an end to Carrot's brilliantly stupid plan.
