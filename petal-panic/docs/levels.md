# Petal Panic — Level Design

## Level 1 — The Circus

**Boss:** Tusko Wobble

Warm, lively vintage circus. Scarlet and Balthazar discover something has corrupted their home. Starts playful and gradually introduces the madness.

**Vibe:** Nostalgic big-top warmth turning unsettling. Music box waltzes detuning, calliope organ playing slightly wrong notes.

**Enemies:** Jester, Jack-O-Lantern, Vine Hound, Boris Loon (babies)

**Environment:** Big top interior, sawdust floors, striped tents, hanging lamps, crowd silhouettes in background.

---

## Level 2 — Carnival After Dark

**Boss:** Ratchet Rumbelow

The adventure spills outside into a huge carnival. More open, colorful and chaotic, but increasingly creepy as they get deeper.

**Vibe:** Neon-lit funhouse at midnight. Calliope music warped, ferris wheel creaking, calliope organs playing minor keys.

**Enemies:** Jester, Boris Loon, Ratzo the Ringleader, Toadstool Tilly, Doodle Dink

**Environment:** Carnival grounds, game booths, ferris wheel, carousel, popcorn stands, fading lights, fog.

---

## Level 3 — The Pirate Ship

**Boss:** Grim Vertigo

They take to sea toward Carrot's territory. Stormy skies, enormous wooden ship, rocking decks, cannons, ropes and dangerous vertical sections. The adventure becomes much more intense.

**Vibe:** Storm-tossed deck, crashing waves, creaking wood. Ominous brass, thunder, ship bells.

**Enemies:** Gustav Grapplersnout, Toadstool Tilly, Vine Hound, Boris Loon, Doodle Dink

**Environment:** Wooden ship decks, rigging, cannons, barrels, storm clouds, lightning, rocking platforms, vertical rope/ladder sections.

---

## Level 4 — Carrot's Republic

**Boss:** Colonel Carrot (final)

They arrive in Colonel Carrot's absurd authoritarian world. Streets become grander and more oppressive until they reach his palace/headquarters. Big spectacle, increasingly ridiculous propaganda, then the final confrontation with Carrot.

**Vibe:** Absurd totalitarian circus. Marching bands playing propaganda jingles, giant carrot banners, forced-cheering crowds, garish red/gold everywhere.

**Enemies:** All enemies (gated spawns), heavy mix of Ratzo, Gustav, Doodle Dink, Toadstool Tilly

**Environment:** Propaganda streets → grand boulevard → carrot palace exterior → throne room. Giant carrot statues, "OBEY THE CARROT" banners, forced-smile crowd puppets, golden scooter thrones.
