# Grim Vertigo

**Role:** Boss — Carnival Night; corrupted carousel  
**Type:** Living carousel  

Once the pride of the midnight carnival, Grim Vertigo was a dazzling carousel of painted horses, bright lights and impossible music. Carrot’s botanical contamination gave the attraction a monstrous life of its own. Now its riders are a bizarre collection of snarling circus creatures, and every spin grows faster and more dangerous. Grim Vertigo has no grand ambition or allegiance: it is a beloved attraction gone terribly wrong, turning the carnival’s happiest memories into a dizzying nightmare.
