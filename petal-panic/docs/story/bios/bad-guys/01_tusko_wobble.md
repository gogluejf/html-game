# Tusko Wobble

**Role:** Boss — Big Top; corrupted circus elephant  
**Type:** Elephant  

A beloved giant of the Canadian circus, Tusko Wobble once delighted audiences with spectacular tricks and a surprisingly delicate sense of balance. Everything changed when Colonel Carrot’s experimental botanical weapon struck the Big Top. Flowers and tangled vines erupted across his body, turning the gentle performer into a furious, unstoppable force. Beneath the rampage, the old Tusko is still there. Scarlet and Balthazhar must survive his thunderous charge without forgetting that he is one of their own.
