# The Great Maraboo

**Role:** Boss — Wild Menagerie; corrupted bird performer  
**Type:** Bird  

The Great Maraboo always believed he deserved the biggest stage, the loudest applause and the finest costume in the circus. After the botanical disaster reached the forgotten menagerie, his already enormous ego grew alongside his monstrous wings and talons. He proclaimed himself ruler of the abandoned cages and overgrown wagons, demanding that every creature admire his magnificent performance. Loud, vain and wildly theatrical, Maraboo attacks like every battle is his final encore.
