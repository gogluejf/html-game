# Ironhoof

**Role:** Boss — Runaway Train; railway commander  
**Type:** Armored horse  

Ironhoof is Colonel Carrot’s iron-fisted commander of railway security, a towering armored horse who believes every problem can be solved by charging straight through it. He rules the locomotives, tunnels and mountain tracks with military precision—or at least his own extremely loud version of it. His enormous metal hooves shake entire carriages, and his temper is as explosive as the engines he guards. Unlike the corrupted circus victims, Ironhoof works for Carrot willingly, convinced that brute force is the highest form of authority.
