# Madame Beetroot

**Role:** Boss — Veggie City; Carrot’s calculating second-in-command  
**Type:** Beetroot  

Madame Beetroot is the undisputed diva of Veggie City: a jewel-covered, fur-draped aristocrat whose every entrance deserves an orchestra. She adores applause, luxury and the sound of her own voice, but behind the theatrical vanity hides the intelligence that keeps Colonel Carrot’s chaotic empire functioning. She lets the self-proclaimed mastermind enjoy his speeches while quietly handling the decisions that matter. Scarlet and Balthazhar discover that the closer they get to the palace, the more dangerous the people around Carrot become.
