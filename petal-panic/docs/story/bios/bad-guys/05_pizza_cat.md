# Pizza Cat

**Role:** Boss — Freakshow; Carrot’s recruited enforcer  
**Type:** Cat — pizza-mutated monster  

Pizza Cat has the strangest origin of any creature in the Freakshow: he was the very first monster imagined with Midjourney, back in the early days of AI-generated imagery. An impossible fusion of furious feline and dripping pizza, he is living proof that some ideas should never have escaped the picture. In the world of Petal Panic, this absurd celebrity became the Freakshow’s star attraction before Colonel Carrot recruited him to keep unwanted visitors away. Pizza Cat does not care about grand plans. He cares about attention, territory and tearing intruders to pieces.
