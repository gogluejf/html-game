# Ratchet Rumbelow

**Role:** Boss — Pirate Show; opportunistic pirate-show villain  
**Type:** Unknown  

Ratchet Rumbelow was trouble long before Colonel Carrot’s botanical experiment. A flamboyant menace of the traveling pirate show, he treats every confrontation like a chance to steal the spotlight. When chaos sweeps through the circus world, Ratchet sees opportunity rather than danger. Armed with his outrageous balloon bat and a talent for turning any deck into a battlefield, he blocks Scarlet and Balthazhar’s ocean crossing simply because ruining their plans sounds entertaining. He serves himself, not Carrot.
