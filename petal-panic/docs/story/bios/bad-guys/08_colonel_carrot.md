# Colonel Carrot

**Role:** Villain — self-proclaimed criminal mastermind  
**Type:** Carrot  
**IQ: 23** — *Somehow commands an army despite being spectacularly stupid.*

Wanted for an impressive collection of crimes, failed schemes and suspicious botanical experiments, Colonel Carrot has one modest ambition: absolute domination. From his palace on the mysterious Great Continent, he unleashes an experimental botanical weapon on a traveling circus in Canada. One bloom later, the Big Top is overrun by mutated plants, corrupted performers and vegetable henchmen. Carrot believes this proves his unmatched genius. Unfortunately for his empire, his greatest weakness is the one thing no weapon can fix: Carrot himself.
