# Ringo Wonderwick

**Role:** Circus owner — ringmaster and mentor  
**IQ: 133** — *A perceptive showman with a sharp instinct for people and strategy.*

Ringo Wonderwick has spent his life keeping the magic of the circus alive. A charismatic, eccentric showman, he acquired a struggling circus after its journey from Romania to Canada in the wake of the Great Depression.

He recognized Scarlet's extraordinary talent and intelligence, becoming her mentor and giving her the freedom to develop into the performer she wanted to be. When Balthazhar arrived, Ringo quickly realized that the tiger was no ordinary animal.

Behind the grand gestures, colorful costumes, and theatrical speeches is a protective leader who considers the performers his family. Ringo often knows more than he lets on.
