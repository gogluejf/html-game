# Bruno Strongheart

**Role:** Circus strongman — the gentle giant  
**IQ: 113** — *Practical, resourceful, and surprisingly clever when it matters.*

Born with extraordinary physical strength, Bruno Strongheart spent much of his childhood accidentally breaking things and frightening people who didn't know him. Despite his enormous size, Bruno was a gentle soul who wanted nothing more than to make people smile.

Everything changed when Ringo Wonderwick welcomed him into the circus. For the first time, Bruno's unusual strength was celebrated rather than feared. He quickly became a crowd favorite, effortlessly lifting enormous weights and performing seemingly impossible feats.

Fiercely loyal to his circus family, Bruno considers Scarlet and Balthazhar his closest friends. He may not always follow their complicated plans, but when trouble comes, he's usually the first to jump in and help.
