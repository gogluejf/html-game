# Madame Zappy

**Role:** Circus merchant — power-up and equipment specialist  
**IQ: 141** — *A brilliant entrepreneur with an eye for invention and opportunity.*

Madame Zappy has always had a talent for turning dangerous inventions into profitable merchandise. Clever, flamboyant, and fast-talking, she never quite fit into conventional business circles—especially when her inventory included explosive juggling balls and experimental energy boosters.

Ringo Wonderwick offered her a place where her eccentric merchandise could finally find an appreciative audience. She set up shop with the circus and quickly became its go-to supplier of ammunition, shields, energy boosters, and increasingly questionable upgrades.

Zappy cares deeply about Scarlet, Balthazhar, and the rest of the troupe. That doesn't mean anyone gets a discount.
