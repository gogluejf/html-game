# Pip the Mechanic

**Role:** Circus mechanic — inventor and vehicle specialist  
**Type:** Android  
**IQ: 103** — *Mechanically gifted, resourceful, and endlessly curious.*

Nobody knows exactly who built Pip or where he originally came from. The little android was discovered among discarded mechanical equipment backstage at a circus that had gone bankrupt during the Great Depression. Damaged and seemingly beyond repair, he had been left behind with the rest of the abandoned machinery.

Ringo Wonderwick brought him into the circus, where Pip eventually managed to repair himself using spare parts and forgotten machinery.

Since then, Pip has become the circus's indispensable mechanic, maintaining everything from stage equipment to the infamous clown-faced bumper car. Cheerful, curious, and occasionally overconfident, Pip approaches every mechanical problem with enthusiasm. His repairs aren't always conventional, and his inventions sometimes create more problems than they solve, but his loyalty to his circus family is beyond question.
