# Scarlet Vale

**Role:** Hero — aerialist, co-star of the circus  
**IQ: 164** — *Brilliant, inventive, and an exceptionally quick thinker.*

Scarlet Vale has no memory of her parents or family. Adopted by a circus owner in Romania, she grew up beneath the big top and learned the trapeze before most children learned to stay out of trouble.

After the Great Depression, the circus crossed the Atlantic to Canada, where Ringo Wonderwick acquired it and became Scarlet's mentor. Brilliant, rebellious, and almost impossible to intimidate, Scarlet grew into the circus's fearless star aerialist. Eventually, she met the one performer even harder to control than herself: Balthazhar.

She never tried to tame him. She treated him as a partner. He approved.
