# Balthazhar

**Role:** Hero — tiger ringmaster, co-star of the circus
**Species:** Tiger
**IQ: 147** — *Freakishly intelligent; understands human speech and strategy.*

Born wild in the foothills of Nepal, Balthazhar survived poachers only to be sold into the animal trade in India. After passing through several hands and circuses, the rebellious tiger eventually landed under the big top in Canada.

He hated tricks, trainers and being told what to do. Then he met Scarlet. She understood him, respected him, and most importantly, never tried to tame him. Balthazhar chose her as his partner and refused to perform for anyone else.

Together, they became the circus's most unlikely star duo.
