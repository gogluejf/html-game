# Petal Panic — Level Storyline

Each level is part of one continuous physical journey. Every level ends by revealing, causing, or providing the path into the next location.

---

## Opening Cinematic — Operation Petal Panic

Deep inside a grand laboratory, Colonel Carrot unveils his latest invention: an experimental botanical weapon. Before his assembled vegetable soldiers, the self-proclaimed criminal mastermind announces his plan for absolute domination.

He slams a giant red button. Nothing happens. He presses it again. Still nothing. Furious, he kicks the machine.

**BOOM!** An enormous glowing bloom erupts, launching a strange botanical payload into the night sky.

Far away in Canada, Scarlet and Balthazhar are performing beneath the Big Top. Ringo introduces his stars to a cheering crowd. Bruno prepares backstage, Pip tinkers with machinery, and Madame Zappy tends her spectacular shop.

The bloom crashes into the circus and bursts. Pollen fills the air. Props spring to life, plants mutate, and performers turn hostile. The audience scatters as the Big Top descends into chaos.

Scarlet and Balthazhar exchange a look. Their home is under attack.

**GAME START.**

---

# Level 1 — Big Top

**Boss:** Tusko Wobble

**Vibe:** Warm, nostalgic 1930s circus magic slowly turning strange and unsettling. It should still feel like home beneath the corruption.

**Notorious Elements:** Giant striped circus tent, main performance ring, hanging spotlights, red velvet curtains, trapezes, audience silhouettes.

Scarlet and Balthazhar fight through their corrupted home and confront **Tusko Wobble**.

### Ending / Transition

The confrontation tears open the rear of the Big Top. Bruno holds up fallen wreckage while the heroes escape. Ringo urges them to follow the spreading corruption, and Pip insists on coming along with his tools.

Beyond the torn canvas, the carnival glows beneath the night sky.

→ **Carnival Night**

---

# Level 2 — Carnival Night

**Boss:** Grim Vertigo

**Vibe:** Magical midnight carnival turning surreal, with beautiful lights and an unsettling sense that something is wrong.

**Notorious Elements:** Ferris wheel, Grim Vertigo's carousel, carnival booths, strings of lights, balloons, popcorn stands, enormous moon.

Scarlet and Balthazhar pursue the chaos through the carnival's attractions and defeat **Grim Vertigo**, the corrupted carousel. Madame Zappy provides weapon support over the radio.

### Ending / Transition

The carousel stops. Beyond the last lights, an old iron gate opens onto abandoned animal wagons, broken cages and wild vegetation. Pip follows with his equipment ( just shy behind ).

→ **Wild Menagerie**

---

# Level 3 — Wild Menagerie

**Boss:** The Great Maraboo

**Vibe:** Exotic circus wilderness, adventurous and mysterious rather than dark.

**Notorious Elements:** Animal cages, circus wagons, jungle vegetation, old animal posters, abandoned kiosks, exotic plants.

The heroes cross the overgrown animal grounds and confront **The Great Maraboo**.

### Ending / Transition

Beyond the last cages, they hear gulls, foghorns and ship bells. From high ground they spot a circus port and a pirate show ship preparing to depart. The trail leads across the sea toward the mysterious **Great Continent**.

Ringo checks in by radio. Pip helps the heroes reach the docks before the ship leaves.

→ **Pirate Show**

---

# Level 4 — Pirate Show

**Boss:** Ratchet Rumbelow

**Vibe:** Grand theatrical pirate adventure becoming a dangerous, stormy ocean voyage.

**Notorious Elements:** Giant wooden circus ship, striped circus sails, cannons, rigging, enormous waves, moonlit ocean, lightning.

Scarlet, Balthazhar and Pip board the ship just in time. The pirate performance turns into a wild crossing, culminating in a battle with **Ratchet Rumbelow**. Pip struggles to keep the ship's machinery running.

### Ending / Transition

The battered ship reaches the opposite coast. Through the fog, music plays and lights flicker on. A crooked sign announces **FREAKSHOW**.

Their first steps onto the Great Continent lead straight into it.

→ **Freakshow**

---

# Level 5 — Freakshow

**Boss:** Pizza Cat

**Vibe:** Spooky, grotesque and funny — a vintage sideshow nightmare that remains unmistakably Petal Panic.

**Notorious Elements:** Freakshow banners, specimen jars, muscle-freak posters, crooked tents, strange lamps, skull decorations, Pizza Cat imagery.

Scarlet and Balthazhar battle through bizarre attractions and face the show's star, **Pizza Cat**. Madame Zappy checks in with fresh weapon support.

### Ending / Transition

Behind the Freakshow, the heroes discover a small railway station. A destination board reads **VEGGIE CITY**, but the last train is already pulling away.

Pip gets a neglected rail cart moving just long enough to help them catch the departing train. They leap aboard as it heads into the mountains.

→ **Runaway Train**

---

# Level 6 — Runaway Train

**Boss:** Ironhoof

**Vibe:** Relentless forward motion, exhilarating and slightly out of control.

**Notorious Elements:** Circus locomotive, train rooftops, smoke stacks, mountain ranges, tunnels, huge bridges, rapidly scrolling landscape.

The train accelerates wildly through the mountains. Scarlet and Balthazhar fight across the carriages toward **Ironhoof**, while Pip tries to understand what's gone wrong with the locomotive.

### Ending / Transition

Ironhoof falls. The train bursts out of the mountains, revealing an enormous city below and a palace dominating its skyline.

**Veggie City.** Colonel Carrot is finally within reach.

→ **Veggie City**

---

# Level 7 — Veggie City

**Boss:** Madame Beetroot

**Vibe:** Beautiful, absurd and slightly spooky vegetable metropolis, growing grander and stranger toward the palace.

**Notorious Elements:** Vegetable markets, crooked city buildings, carrot monuments, bridges, vegetable citizens, grand boulevard, distant Carrot Palace.

Scarlet and Balthazhar cross Colonel Carrot's capital, passing markets, monuments and extravagant displays of his rule before confronting **Madame Beetroot**. Ringo makes one final radio call from the circus.

### Ending / Transition

Madame Beetroot gives her final performance. At the far end of the grand boulevard, the gates of **Carrot Palace** open. The heroes enter, and the doors slam shut behind them. Pip remains outside to keep their escape route ready.

→ **Carrot Palace**

---

# Level 8 — Carrot Palace

**Boss:** Colonel Carrot

**Vibe:** Monumental, theatrical and completely excessive — the absurdity of the journey at its peak.

**Notorious Elements:** Giant carrot statues, monumental halls, red-and-gold banners, throne room, absurd portraits of Colonel Carrot, palace guards, enormous windows overlooking Veggie City.

Scarlet and Balthazhar climb through the palace toward the source of the disaster. At its heart waits the self-proclaimed mastermind himself: **Colonel Carrot**.

### Finale

Colonel Carrot falls. His botanical weapon is destroyed, and the corruption begins to disappear.

The ending retraces the journey in reverse:

**Veggie City → Mountains → Freakshow Coast → Ocean → Wild Menagerie → Carnival Night → Big Top**

Lights return. Performers recover. The carnival comes back to life.

Back in Canada, Ringo, Bruno and Madame Zappy welcome Scarlet, Balthazhar and Pip home. The circus is theirs again.
