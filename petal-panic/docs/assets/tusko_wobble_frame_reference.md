## CHARACTER SHEET: TUSKO WOBBLE (BOSS)

Source frame: `petal-panic/assets/boss/tusko_wobble_charge_f1.png`

**Identity (who he is, independent of any pose)**
- Massive anthropomorphic war-elephant boss: bulky quadrupedal body with a feline-like head, thick trunk, and huge ears.
- Overwhelming, tanky physique: enormous barrel chest, thick pillar legs, short neck, wide shoulders. Reads as a walking fortress.
- Fierce, battle-ready expression: bared teeth, narrowed angry eyes, flared nostrils.

**Locked palette (this character)**
- Fur/hide base: grey-blue `#6E7A85`, shadow `#4A545E`, deep shadow `#2E353C`.
- Tusks/teeth: cream-bone `#EFE6D0`, shadow `#B8AC9C`.
- Trunk: grey-blue matching hide, darker crease lines.
- Saddle/armor: deep red `#8E1E2C`, dark fold `#5E0E18`, gold trim/studs `#C9A227`.
- Floral crown: red blossoms `#D40A2C`, orange buds `#F07818`, green leaves `#3E7A2E`, dark leaf `#1E4A18`.
- Shoulder pauldron: red `#A02838`, gold rivets `#C9A227`.
- Claw/toe nails: cream-white `#F4EDE0`.

**Head & face**
- Broad elephant head with a thick curling trunk hanging forward, ending in a slightly open tip.
- Large fan-shaped ears with inner ear detail in a darker shade.
- Short curved tusks sweeping forward from either side of the trunk base.
- Narrowed angry eyes, flared nostrils, bared lower teeth — fierce war-mongering expression.
- A crown of tropical flowers (red blossoms, orange buds, green leaves) sits atop the head between the ears.

**Body & legs**
- Enormous barrel torso covered by a decorated saddle/armor piece.
- Four thick pillar legs ending in large flat feet with three visible cream claw/toe nails each.
- A short tail with a tufted end visible behind the body.

**Costume details (must stay identical across frames)**
- Red leather saddle draped over the back, edged with gold trim and studded with gold dots.
- A single red shoulder pauldron with gold rivets on the near-side shoulder.
- Tropical flower crown on the head (red/orange/green cluster).
- Thin gold chain or strap detail running along the saddle edge.

**Recognition anchors (do not move/redraw per frame)**
Flower crown, saddle shape + gold studs, shoulder pauldron, tusk curve, ear shape, trunk length.
