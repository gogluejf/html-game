## CHARACTER SHEET: SCARLET VALE

Source frame: `petal-panic/assets/heroes/scarlet_vale_idle_f1.png`

**Locked palette (this character)**
- Hair: near-black plum `#1A0E14`, violet sheen `#3A1E33`, magenta-red highlights.
- Primary accent (rose, cape, shoes): red `#D40A2C`, dark `#7E0518`, bright tip `#FF3B5C`.
- Costume base: black `#140A0E` with gold trim/studs `#C9A227`.
- Stockings: semi-transparent black over skin (skin shows through ~40%).
- Mask: matte black with subtle purple ambient bounce.
- Feather plume: off-white `#EFE7DA`, grey underside `#9A8F86`.

**Identity (who she is, independent of any pose)**
- Young woman, athletic hourglass figure: small head, cinched waist, long legs, slim arms and hands.
- Confident, playful expression: closed-lip smile, relaxed arched brows, half-lidded eyes.

**Face**
- Oval face, pointed chin, deep-red full lips, small nose suggested by one short line, long lashes. Eyes are hidden behind the mask eye-holes in most frames.

**Hair**
- Very voluminous curly black bob flaring past both shoulders. A single large white feather plume rises from the top-back of the head; a large five-petal red rose is pinned on the left side of the hair (viewer's right).

**Costume details (must stay identical across frames)**
- Black domino mask covering the eye area, edges blending into the hairline.
- Strapless corset bodice: black base, red heart-shaped panel down the center, gold lacing/studs along seams, sweetheart neckline.
- Matching black hip briefs with a small gold belt/buckle at the front.
- Red cape attached at the shoulders, flowing behind and to the viewer's left, scalloped lower edge.
- Short red petal-skirt layered over the hips, same scalloped edge, shorter than the cape.
- Long black gloves reaching mid-upper-arm, clean straight cuff just below the elbow.
- Thigh-high black stockings with a hard horizontal top line at mid-thigh; bare-skin gap between stocking and brief.
- Pointed-toe red stiletto pumps, ~2-inch heel, glossy finish.

**Recognition anchors (do not move/redraw per frame)**
Rose, feather plume, mask, corset heart, gold studs, stocking top line, shoe shape.
