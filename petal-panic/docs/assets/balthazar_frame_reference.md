## CHARACTER SHEET: BALTHAZAR

Source frame: `petal-panic/assets/heroes/balthazar_idle_f1.png`

**Identity (who he is, independent of any pose)**
- Anthropomorphic tiger gentleman: upright humanoid build with a feline head, paws, and tail.
- Sturdy, well-fed physique: broad chest and shoulders, thick arms and legs, short neck.
- Distinguished, suave expression: closed-lip smile, relaxed half-lidded eyes, raised confident brow.

**Locked palette (this character)**
- Fur base: orange `#F07818`, deep orange shadow `#C2540E`.
- Fur stripes/markings: near-black `#1E1410`.
- Fur white areas (muzzle, chest, belly, paw pads): cream-white `#F4EDE0`, grey shadow `#B8AC9C`.
- Tail: alternating orange and black bands, cream tip.
- Suit: black tuxedo `#161014`, lapel sheen `#2E262C`.
- Shirt: off-white `#EFE9DC`, button dots `#C9A227`.
- Bow tie: red `#C41E3A`, dark fold `#7E0518`.
- Cane: black shaft `#141014`, gold handle and ferrule `#C9A227`.

**Head & face**
- Feline head: rounded skull, small rounded ears with dark interiors, short muzzle.
- Cream-white muzzle and brow patches framing the eyes; black nose; whisker dots on the cheeks.
- Orange fur over the crown and sides with a few short black stripe tufts on the forehead.
- Relaxed half-lidded eyes, arched brow, closed-lip smile on the muzzle.

**Body & paws**
- Humanoid torso covered by the suit; forearms end in large orange tiger paws with cream finger pads and short dark claws.
- Feet are large orange tiger paws with cream toes and dark claw marks.
- Long striped tail (orange/black bands, cream tip) extending behind the body.

**Costume details (must stay identical across frames)**
- Black formal tuxedo jacket: satin peak lapels, single-breasted front, fitted sleeves ending in wide white shirt cuffs.
- White dress shirt with a row of small gold buttons down the center.
- Red bow tie at the collar.
- Black tailored trousers matching the jacket.
- A slim cane held in one hand: black shaft, ornate gold handle and gold foot ferrule.

**Recognition anchors (do not move/redraw per frame)**
Tiger head markings (cream muzzle/brow patches, ear shape), bow tie, gold shirt buttons, lapel shape, cane, tail banding pattern.
